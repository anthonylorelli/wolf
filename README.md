# Vesper Manor — 19th-Century Paranormal Cutaway Adventure (Phaser)

A mobile- and desktop-friendly **2D pixel-art occult side-scroller & mansion-reclamation game** built with **Phaser 4**, set across **22 richly decorated rooms & grounds** in a **4-story cutaway 1889 Victorian steampunk mansion**.

## Highlights

- **Connected Steam Pipes, Electrical Lines & Aetheric Conduits (0-Coin Room Restoration)**: Rooms require **no coins** to restore. Instead, every wing requires live power constructed room-by-room outward from the **Central Foyer Tri-Manifold Dynamo** across ceilings and up/down staircase & lift shafts:
  - **Copper Steam Pipes** — Pressurized riveted copper mains with brass flanges & gauges powering culinary, boiler, conservatory, and grounds wings.
  - **Galvanic Electrical Lines** — Braided copper cables on white porcelain insulators feeding grand halls, labs, clockworks, and subterranean cisterns.
  - **Crystal Aetheric Conduits** — Verdigris occult vacuum tubes feeding ancestral galleries, esoteric libraries, crypts, and the Celestial Observatory.
- **Room-Powered Paranormal Shields (No Physical Barriers)**: Physical wood/iron walls are replaced by shimmering floor-to-ceiling **Paranormal Shields**. Once a room has live power, switch its doorway **Shield Emitter Relay** (`0 Florins`) to project a **Salt-Steam Ward Shield** (knockback), **Galvanic Arc-Shield** (reflection shock damage), or **Astral Resonance Shield** (chill slow + bolt mitigation) blocking enemy entrance!
- **22 Bespoke Named Room Dioramas**: Every single room renders custom multi-prop pixel-art interiors tailored to its exact name—from the *State Banquet & Séance Hall* (banquet table, crystal ball & chandeliers), *Weeping Portrait Colonnade* (bleeding ancestral portraits that heal when powered), *Cast-Iron Scullery & Alembic Kitchen*, and *Two-Tier Esoteric Library* to the *Galvanic & Ectoplasm Lab*, *Clockwork Bellfry*, *Celestial Aether Observatory*, and *Great Copper Boiler Vault*.
- **Choose Your 19th-Century Paranormal Investigator**: Select on the Start Screen between **Mina Vesper** (*Esoteric Medium* with swinging Brass Spirit-Censer) and **Dr. Julian Vesper** (*Psychical Occultist* with crackling Leyden Spirit-Lantern), unleash **Séance Exorcisms**, rally **Housefolk**, and shatter all **4 Spectral Rifts**.

## Controls (iPhone / Touch & Desktop)

| Platform | Stride / Astral-Sprint | Wire Conduits / Switch Shields / Florin | Stairs & Brass Cage Lift | Séance Exorcism | 4-Story Cutaway Overview |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **iPhone / Touch** | Left Brass Stick *(full tilt = Sprint)* | Hold **ACTION** *(tap away = Ward 1)* | Tap **↑ UP / ↓ DN** *(or flick Stick)* | Tap **EXORCISE** | Tap **Overview** / Cutaway Map |
| **Desktop** | `A`/`D` or `←`/`→` (`SHIFT` Sprint) | Hold `E` / `S` / `↓` *(tap = Ward 1)* | `W`/`↑` (Up) & `S`/`↓` (Down) | `SPACE` | `M` (Overview) / `V` (Audio) / `?` (Ledger) |
