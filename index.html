    Inspector’s Field Guide

    1. Banish the Gloom

    Hold BEAM (or SPACE / Right-Click) to focus your Limelight Brass Lantern onto Whispering Shades, Soot Wraiths, and Miasma Pools to dissolve them into Aether Salt.

    2. Salvage & Pressurize

    Walk near rubble piles, cobwebs, and coal crates and tap ACT / Salvage (or E) to gather Brass Fittings and Coal. Repairing boilers and steam pipes raises Estate PSI, unsealing heavy bulkhead doors.

    3. Reclaim Every Wing

    Restore broken Victorian furnishings, relight gas sconces, and ignite each room’s Harmonic Ward. Warm, gaslit rooms restore your Nerve and recharge your lantern automatically.
