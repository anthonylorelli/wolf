1. Sovereign Coin Slots

Pause beside any fixture, rack, campfire, or bulkhead to reveal floating coin slots (ˆ ˆ ˆ). Hold S / ↓ / E (or hold COIN on iPhone) to feed Brass Sovereigns one by one! Tap away from slots to toss a single coin onto the floor to recruit Vagrants or bribe night Shades.

2. Autonomous Retainers

Recruit stranded Vagrants (1 Coin), then stock the Foyer Wrench Rack (Artificers who build Bulkheads & restore haunted rooms) and Lantern Rack (Lamplighter Warders who hunt daytime Soot Wisps for coins & guard your outermost walls at night).

3. 4-Story Cutaway & Lift

Explore 22 asymmetric wings across 4 stories—from the Subterranean Boiler Vaults to the Apex Observatory Spire—using sweeping staircases and the central Brass Cage Lift (W/↑ & S/↓ or ↑ UP / ↓ DN). Press M or tap the Cutaway Map for a live zoomed-out dollhouse view!

4. Night Waves & Rift Assaults

Every night, shades march inward from active Spectral Rifts. Upgrade your Foyer Hearth to Tier II+ to unlock Iron & Brass Bulkheads, the Majordomo Safe, and Strike Squad Banners (4 Coins) to march Warders out and shatter all 4 Rifts!
