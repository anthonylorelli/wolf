1. Scavenge, Haul & Refine

Pry open Haunted Salvage Caches in dark rooms (or banish apparitions) for Brass Scrap & Raw Ectoplasm. Manor Porters haul heavy crates across stairs & the Brass Lift to the Foyer Supply Depot, where the Workbench refines them into Conduit Kits!

2. Conduit Kits & Power Lines

Each room’s Junction Terminal requires 1–3 Refined Kits: Steam Pipe Kits, Galvanic Wire Spools, or Refined Aether Phials. Hold E/S to slot Kits & weld the conduit, or queue the Junction so Artificers forge & carry Kits from the Foyer!

3. Room-Powered Paranormal Shields

Once a room has live power, hold E/S at its doorway Shield Emitter Relay (0 Florins) to project a Salt-Steam Ward (knockback), Galvanic Arc-Shield (shock), or Astral Resonance Shield (chill slow) blocking enemy entrance!

4. Workshops, Florins & Rifts

Energizing specialized wings like the Boiler Vault, Telegraph Hub, Galvanic Lab & Wintergarden unlocks 2× Workshop refining and Dawn supply yields. Spend Silver Florins on Staff, Tools, Dynamo Tiers & Banishment Squads!
