Inspector’s Field Guide

1. Brass Wrench Action

Swing your Brass Wrench (Click / J / E or WRENCH) to smash crates into magnetic Brass Gears, repair broken boilers & sconces on contact, splinter wooden barricades, and deflect ecto-orbs back at spirits for massive critical hits!

2. Calcium Limelight Tether

Hold BEAM (or drag the BEAM button to aim 360° on iPhone; SPACE / Right-Click on desktop) to lock crackling golden limelight onto shades, poltergeist armchair mimics, and furnace brutes!

3. 19 Asymmetric Wings & Secrets

Repairing steam pipes and copper boilers raises Estate PSI, automatically unsealing pressure bulkheads across the manor. Watch walls for gleaming golden books—a Wrench swing opens hidden revolving bookcases!

4. Fast-Travel & Upgrades

Reclaiming a room lights its gas sconces permanently and links it for 1-tap Fast-Travel on the Map (M). Open Craft (U) anytime to forge permanent tool upgrades.
